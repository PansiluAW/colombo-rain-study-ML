A compiled local dataset

4,383 daily records from the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API, using ECMWF ERA5. The extract covers 1 January 2014 to 31 December 2025 in the Asia/Colombo time zone. It was collected on 27 October 2026.

Dates are consecutive and complete. The first six incomplete history windows and final unavailable target were removed. Rainfall inputs use log(1 + rain); scaling uses training statistics only. The source request, units and checksum are included in data/provenance.json.

Where this study stops

These are gridded estimates, rather than measurements at a Colombo weather station. One grid cell misses local variation. Wet days are common, and a 1 mm boundary creates a sharp distinction between small rainfall amounts.

ERA5 is delayed reanalysis. This experiment checks historical rain-day patterns and does not establish real-time forecast skill. Station checks, inputs available at forecast time, calibration and wider geographic testing would be needed for practical use.

No personal data were collected. Data by Open-Meteo, using ECMWF ERA5, under CC BY 4.0. Daily JSON was converted to CSV; lagged, rolling and seasonal predictors were derived.
